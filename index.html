ABOUT NERV JAPAN

Origins (GEHIRN to NERV)

Established in 2010 as the direct successor to the artificial evolution research institute GEHIRN following the cataclysmic Second Impact in 2000. Its dissolution and restructuring concentrated mankind's most advanced biomechanical engineering under unified executive command.

Headquarters (Tokyo-3 & GeoFront)

Anchored deep beneath the fortified city of Tokyo-3 inside the vast subterranean GeoFront cavern in Hakone, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan. The inverted pyramid complex houses command bridges, Evangelion cages, and the MAGI computational core.

Core Mandate

Operating as a special paramilitary agency under the United Nations while secretly directed by SEELE, NERV is tasked with deploying Evangelion units to intercept and neutralize Angels while covertly overseeing the Human Instrumentality Project.
